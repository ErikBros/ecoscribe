# Ecoscribe user guides

Ecoscribe types what you say into any app and takes notes in your calls, on Windows and macOS. Everything runs on
your own computer: no account, no subscription, and no audio or text leaves it (the only exceptions are the ones
you switch on yourself, listed in each guide).

## Getting started

Download, install, first-run setup, where your files are.

- [On Windows](windows.md)
- [On a Mac](mac.md)

## The window

Open it from the tray icon (Mac: the menu bar icon) or the Start menu. **Home** has the state ("Ready"), today's
numbers, your last dictations, Record a meeting and Transcribe a file, and the cards that need you (a new version, a
crash report, your to-dos). **Meetings**, **History**, **Insights** (when switched on) and **Settings** are the other
tabs.
