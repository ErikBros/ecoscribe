# Insights and speech coaching

([All guides](README.md).) Settings > Dictation > Behaviour > **Insights tab** adds a tab with what your dictations
say about how you talk. Everything is worked out on your computer from your history; nothing is sent anywhere. Pick
**7 days**, **30 days** or **All** at the top.

## At a glance

- **Typing saved:** the minutes you'd have spent typing the same words. Set **Your typing speed** (40 words a minute
  is average; 70+ if you type all day) so it matches you.
- Words, dictations, minutes of talking, your day streak; **when you dictate**, words over the last 14 days, your
  languages, and **where you dictate** (which apps).
- **Phrases you repeat**, and filler words across all your dictations.
- **Words to add:** names you said at least twice that aren't in Your words yet. Fix the spelling if needed and add
  one: Ecoscribe leans toward it from then on, in dictations and meetings.
- **Your week for Claude:** everything you dictated in the last 7 days as one prompt (**Copy for Claude**), for a
  summary of your themes, decisions and loose ends.

## How clearly you speak

After each dictation Ecoscribe takes a second look at how sure it was of every word (a fraction of a second, after
the paste). You see the share of words heard clearly, week by week, and the words it keeps struggling with, per
language. It fills from your next dictations on.

## In your messages to people

Hedges ("maybe", "I think", "kind of") and filler words ("like", "basically") per 100 words, week by week, in what you
dictate to people (chat, email), not in prompts to AI apps. Filler words are normal in speech; in a message, hedges
can read as unsure.

**Note filler words after a message** (Settings > Dictation > Behaviour): right after you dictate a message or an email
to someone, the pill shows the filler words and hedges you used.

## Pace and pauses

Words a minute and how much of the time you pause, to people and to AI apps. Listeners follow most easily at about
130-160 words a minute: that's information, not a goal.

## In your meetings

Your share of the talking, times you talked for over 90 seconds, the questions you asked, and hedges per 100 words,
meeting by meeting. Needs recorded meetings with the call's sound.
