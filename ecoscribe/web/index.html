            Languages you dictate in

            Each dictation, Ecoscribe picks among these by itself, and the pill shows which one it heard. Wrong guess? Hold Right Ctrl and press L to choose the next dictation's language (or the tray icon: Next dictation in). With Swedish (or most added languages) ticked, dictation uses the larger model: still under a second, about twice as slow.

            English and Swedish are built in. Added languages also appear for meetings and files; nothing is downloaded.

            Your words

            Names and terms Ecoscribe tends to get wrong, written the way you want them, one per line. It listens for them, so "Ecoscribe" comes out as Ecoscribe, not "eco scribe". For dictation and meetings.

            Replace

            When Ecoscribe writes the words on the left, it writes the ones on the right instead. In dictations and meeting transcripts, in any case, as whole words.

            Snippets

            Say a snippet's name on its own ("my email.") and Ecoscribe types the saved text instead. Inside a sentence ("what's my email?") it stays as you said it.

            Meetings

            When a call app (Teams, Slack, Zoom, Meet…) starts using the mic.

            Name meetings from your calendar Paste your calendar's secret address in iCal format (Google Calendar: Settings, your calendar, Integrate calendar). A recorded call takes the name of the event happening then, and its attendees help spell names. Read on this PC only; leave empty to turn it off.
            Claude app Let the Claude desktop app read your meetings: "summarize yesterday's call", "what did Ana say about the budget?". Read-only, through a small server on this PC.

            Behaviour

            See all commands
            What it doesEnglishSpanishSwedish

            A command only counts on its own: at the start, or after a pause or punctuation, and before one or the end. "We need a new line of products" stays text. Saying only "send it" presses Enter on what's already in the box.

            Speech models

            Ecoscribe picks the model for each language by itself. Both are downloaded once and then run on this computer, offline. Delete one to free space: it downloads again when it's next needed.
